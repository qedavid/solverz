# I22 Ma's lemma at its edge: the contraction below a Ma quotient is divisible by p (none)

Where C21 stops short of its bound (p^b ≤ 2c), its divisibility step still holds: on H = ZMod(r p) × K the coset
sums agree mod p^b along the subgroup P of order p. Contracting once more, to H/P = ZMod r × K, every coset sum is
then divisible by p, and the autocorrelation λ·p c of those sums must be divisible by p²: p ∣ λc (C33). With a
cyclic Sylow p-subgroup of order ≥ p² and p² ∣ n this reads p ∣ λ.
