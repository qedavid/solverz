# I18 Self-conjugate primes enlarge A7's multiplier group (none)

A7 (C10) searches coset sums on H fixed by M = ∩_{p ∣ n} ⟨p mod exp H⟩. A prime of n self-conjugate mod exp H need not
lie in that intersection: C25 lets M′ ignore it, often 2–4 times larger, with far fewer orbits, and C5's congruence
prunes the search further. Rows: 41 kills on 462 rows with M′ ⊋ M; 25 that no other line reaches
(main folder `scratch/ideator/2026-11-06-2034-none.md`'s `none/n1_only.txt`, e.g. N9541, N9543 on C₉₉, N22696 on C₁₄₅, N42350 on C₆₅).
**Lean.** C25 on top of `MultiplierWitt.lean` and C5's congruence; rows through A7's checker (`CosetSearch.lean`)
with M′ in place of M and the congruence as a filter.
**Technique.** `T438` Multiplier theorems and character sums for nonexistence.
**Source.** ideator round 3 (N1); Miller, arXiv:2511.10231, Thm 7.
**Needs.** C5, C10, C25.
**Kill test.** A refuter on C25's S₂ step (that C5's congruence holds with the same r for every coset, and the bracket is an
integer divisible by p^(2j)); a brute-force rerun of the lemma on two more (H, n, μ) with solutions.
