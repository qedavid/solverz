# I23 Line tomography on Z_p × Z_p: all quotients at once (none)

On G = Z_p × Z_p every x ≠ 0 lies on exactly one of the p + 1 lines, so the coset-sum vectors Yᵢ on the p + 1 line quotients
determine D: Σᵢ Yᵢ(πᵢ x) = p·D(x) + s. A row dies when no choice of valid Yᵢ (each meets C3/C4's demands) makes
Σᵢ Yᵢ(πᵢ x) − s ≡ 0 (mod p) with values in {−p, 0, p}; integrality mod p alone already decides the kills found. Pairs of quotients
are provably too weak on most core rows (D15x); the whole family of lines is not.
