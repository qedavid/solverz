# I23 Line tomography on Z_p × Z_p: all quotients at once (none)

On G = Z_p × Z_p every x ≠ 0 lies on exactly one of the p + 1 lines, so the coset-sum vectors Yᵢ on the p + 1 line quotients
determine D: Σᵢ Yᵢ(πᵢ x) = p·D(x) + s. A row dies when no choice of valid Yᵢ (each meets C3/C4's demands) makes
Σᵢ Yᵢ(πᵢ x) − s ≡ 0 (mod p) with values in {−p, 0, p}; integrality mod p alone already decides the kills found. Pairs of quotients
are provably too weak on most core rows (D15x); the whole family of lines is not.

**Technique.** — tomography (reconstruct D from all its line contractions).
**Source.** us (D15x, 2026-11-10).
**Needs.** C3, C4 (the contractions' demands), C2 (translation, negation), a claim for the line identity.
**Kill test.** Done 2026-11-10 (`scratch/d15x/report.md`): validated on all 229 `exists` rows (every `exists` C_p² row with p ≤ 7 is
found and rechecked; all 10 known-`none` C_p² rows with p ≤ 7 are killed); kills N346 (49,25,2), N348 (49,25,3), N354 (49,33,4) in
Z₇ × Z₇, three independent implementations agreeing. Next: p ≥ 11 (Rust port of `tomo_alg`), the literature check, a refuter round,
then Lean (design in the report). Extensions: C₅³ and non-cyclic G via cyclic quotients.
**Status.** alive.
