# I24 Coset-type counts and the Ma tower at and beyond Ma's edge (none)

On a Ma quotient H = C_{p^j} × K (b = ⌊v_p(n)/2⌋, n′ = n/p^(2b)) C21's step holds at every level H_t = C_{p^(j−t)} × K: the coset sums
Y_t agree mod p^b along u_t (F1), and Lagrange on each u_t-coset with C3's square sums at H_t and H_{t+1} gives
Σ_cosets Σ_{i<i′} ((y_i − y_i′)/p^b)² = (p − 1)·n′ (F2). So a level's cosets fall into a few value types whose counts must meet the
sum, both square sums, Σ|y| ≤ k with its parity and the marking bound; an infeasible count system is a kill (its LP Farkas weights
are an integer certificate). With n′ = 1 each level has exactly one non-constant coset with one outlier ±p^b (the "Ma tower"), so
Y_0 is Y_j on K plus j choices.
