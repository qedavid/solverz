# C36 The divided contraction two levels below a Ma quotient

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G, H = ZMod(r p p) × K a C21 quotient (p prime, r a power of p, p^i h = −h on K, p^e ∣ n,
2 ≤ b ≤ e/2) with fibres of size c, and W the coset sums along G → ZMod r × K (fibres p² c). Then p² divides W, and
Z = W/p² is an integer vector on ZMod r × K with |Z| ≤ c, ΣZ² = Q (p⁴Q = k + λ(p²c − 1)), autocorrelation t (p²t = λc)
at every shift ≠ 0, ΣZ = ±s' (p² s' = s) and an absolute sum A with p²A ≤ k, k − p²A even. If no such vector exists, no
SDS exists. Unlike C34, p = 2 is allowed: a list of bounds k' covers every possible A (one bound for odd p, two for p = 2).

**Uses.** C21's step at both levels (`Ma.dvd_contract_sub`, generalized to `Ma.dvd_contract_quot_sub`: one level down the
coset sum is p times the one above, mod p^b), C3, C34's `false_of_noVectorQ'`.
