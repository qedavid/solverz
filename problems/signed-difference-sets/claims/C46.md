# C46 Coset-type counts on a Ma quotient

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G with s = ΣD, φ : G → H = ZMod(p^j) × K onto a Ma quotient (C21's hypotheses: p^b ∣ n = k − λ, p
self-conjugate on K), c = v/|H|, Y = contract φ D, and u of order p in the cyclic factor. Every fibre of H → H/⟨u⟩ is {h + i·u : i < p},
and C21's step makes its p values pairwise ≡ mod p^b. Each such coset's defect p·Σy² − (Σy)² is ≥ 0 and the defects sum to
(p − 1)(k − λ). C3 on H and on H/⟨u⟩, Σ|Y| ≤ k and the marking bound Σ maxMarked ≥ k then give linear conditions on the cosets; if
integer weights make every admissible coset's weighted row ≥ 0 while the total is negative (an LP Farkas certificate), no SDS exists.
