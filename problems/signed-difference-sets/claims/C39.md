# C39 Value counts of the divided vector (no search)

Under C34's hypotheses at level 1 (Z = W/p) or C36's at level 2 (Z = W/p², b ≥ 2), any prime p: Z is an integer vector on H′ with
|Z| ≤ c, ΣZ = ±s′ and ΣZ² = Q. If no counts of integer values in [−c, c] give |H′| values with sum ±s′ and square sum Q, no SDS
exists. (The autocorrelation and the absolute-sum bound are not needed.)

**Uses.** C33's step (`Ma.dvd_contract_quot`, any p), C36's (`Ma.dvd_contract_quot_sub`), C3.
**Evidence.** Exploration (I22 extension 5, `x_ma_iterated`'s count pre-check, validated on all 229 `exists` rows): 52 open rows the
searches had cut, all at p = 2: R43's 24 at level 2 and 28 at level 1 (among them P6's heavy N5887, N6055);
`scratch/maedge/count_kills.txt`. On all 52, |H′|, the sum and the square sum alone admit no counts (`scratch/c38/final52.out`).
**Lean.** `DividedDemands`, `IsSDS.exists_divided_of_dvd`, `IsSDS.exists_divided`, `IsSDS.exists_divided2`, the count search
`countsGo`/`anyCountsMag` (with ΣZ² ≡ ΣZ mod 2), `DividedDemands.false_of_anyCountsMag`, `MaCountCert` (`MaDividedCounts.lean`, built
`--wfail`, 68 s, 2.7 GB); rows `MaCountRows/N<id>.lean` (52, at most 10.5K nodes, most 1; generator `solver/macountrows.py`; settle

(N131, N19 not killed; bad certificates refused). Axioms standard. Not in the root.
