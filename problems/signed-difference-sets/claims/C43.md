# C43 Tomography through the hyperplanes of a Z_p^r quotient

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G with s = ΣD, φ : G → Z_p^r onto (fibres c = v/p^r), and for a list of hyperplane maps h_m : Z_p^r →
Z_p (a "flat": every point lies in the kernels of all N of them or of exactly α) the contractions y_m of D along h_m ∘ φ (fibres
v/p). Then Σ_m y_m(h_m q) = α·s + (N − α)·Z(q) with Z a contraction of D (|Z| ≤ c·|K|, K the flat's common kernel); for all
hyperplanes this is p^(r−1)·W(q) = Σ_f y_f(f·q) − ((p^(r−1) − 1)/(p − 1))·s. After centring (p ∤ s) each y_m lies in the list of
valid vectors on ZMod p with first moment 0; without centring the first r hyperplanes are normalised by a translation. If no choice
of y's from the lists makes every completed flat integral (mod N − α) and in its box (and Σ|W| ≤ k at the leaf, where used), no SDS
exists.
