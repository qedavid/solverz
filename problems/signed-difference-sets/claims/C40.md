# C40 Line tomography on Z_p × Z_p

Let p be prime and D an SDS (p², k, λ) on G = ZMod p × ZMod p with s = ΣD, p ∤ s. For the p + 1 lines (kernels of the maps
ℓ_0(a, b) = b, ℓ_j(a, b) = a + (j − 1)b, j = 1..p), the line contractions Y_j = contract ℓ_j D satisfy
Σ_j Y_j(ℓ_j g) = p·D(g) + s for every g. After a translation (p ∤ s) every Y_j has Σ_t t·Y_j(t) ≡ 0 mod p, so each Y_j lies in
the list W of valid vectors on ZMod p (C3/C4's demands, fibres p) with that first moment. If no choice of Y_0, …, Y_p from W
makes every Σ_j Y_j(ℓ_j g) − s divisible by p (certificate: vectors w whose pushforwards vanish mod p on the lines after
depth d), no SDS exists. With n = k − λ prime, n ≢ 0, 1 mod p, the centred Y_j are fixed by h ↦ n·h (C10), which shrinks W.
