# C45 The Z_2-twist of a self-conjugate quotient: N1575 and N1593

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G, φ : G → ZMod 2 × K onto with |K| odd and 2^i·h = −h on K, 2^(2j) ∣ k − λ; z the contraction of D to K
and d its twist by the Z_2 character. Then z and d are each constant mod 2^j (C5's congruence, `congr_pow_of_autocorr`, on both),
z ± d = 2·W(e, ·), and the sums, the box and the absolute-sum bound hold. For K = Z_3² (2 ≡ −1 mod 3): (90, 18, 2) dies mod 4 (z ≡ 2,
d ≡ 0 with some |d| ≥ 4, so Σ(|z + d| + |z − d|) ≥ 40 > 2k = 36) and (90, 72, 8) mod 8 (z ≡ 4 and some |d| ≥ 8, so some |z ± d| ≥ 12 >
2c = 10). So N1575 (90, 18, 2) and N1593 (90, 72, 8) in their groups have no SDS.
