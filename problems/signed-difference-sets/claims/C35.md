# C35 Field descent along a chain (Schmidt; P18 M2)

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G, n = k − λ > 0, ZMod m a quotient of G with fibres of size c, and m = d₀ → d₁ → … → F a chain
with dᵢ₊₁ = dᵢ/pᵢ, pᵢ prime (the theorem allows any odd pᵢ), pᵢ² ∣ dᵢ (and 8 ∣ dᵢ if pᵢ = 2), rad m ∣ dᵢ₊₁, and for every prime q ∣ n a unit t ≡ 1 + dᵢ/pᵢ (mod dᵢ)
with t ≡ q^e modulo the q-free part of m. Then φ(m)·n ≤ m·F·c².
