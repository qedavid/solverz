# C33 Ma's lemma at its edge forces p ∣ λc

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G, H = ZMod(r p) × K a quotient with fibres of size c, p prime, r a power of p, p^i h = −h on K,
p^e ∣ n = k − λ, 1 ≤ b ≤ e/2. Then p ∣ λc (the Lean also asks ZMod r × K nontrivial, which R40 shows is not needed). In particular: if p² ∣ n and the Sylow
p-subgroup of G is cyclic of order p^j ≥ p², then p ∣ λ (take H = ZMod p^j, K trivial, c = v/p^j prime to p).

**Uses.** C21 (its divisibility step: p^b ∣ Y(h + u) − Y(h), u of order p), C3 (autocorrelation of coset sums).
**Evidence.** Proof: the coset sums W along ZMod(r p) × K → ZMod r × K (fibres p c) each add p values of Y that agree
mod p, so p ∣ W everywhere; at a shift g ≠ 0 their autocorrelation is λ·p c (C3), now divisible by p², so p ∣ λc.
For j = 1 the conclusion already follows from s² = n + λv; the new cases have j ≥ 2.
