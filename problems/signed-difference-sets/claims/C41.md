# C41 Line tomography on a Z_p × Z_p quotient

Let p be prime, D an SDS (v, k, λ) on G with s = ΣD, φ : G → ZMod p × ZMod p onto (v = c·p²), W = contract φ D (|W| ≤ c) and Y_j
the contractions of D along ℓ_j ∘ φ (the p + 1 lines of C40; fibres c·p). Then Σ_j Y_j(ℓ_j g) = p·W(g) + s. If p ∤ s, a translate of D
in G makes every Y_j's first moment vanish mod p; with a multiplier t (t ≢ 0, 1 mod p and t ≡ q^f mod p for every prime q ∣ n,
n = k − λ, p ∤ n; C10) the centred Y_j are fixed by h ↦ t·h. So each Y_j lies in the list L of vectors on ZMod p meeting C3's
demands (fibres c·p) with these normalisations. If no choice of Y_0, …, Y_p from L makes every Σ_j Y_j(ℓ_j g) − s equal p·z with
|z| ≤ c, no SDS exists. Without centring (p ∣ s) the same holds with L listing every valid vector. With φ = id (c = 1) this is C40
with a wider multiplier condition.
