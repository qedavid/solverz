# C32 Field descent, one step at an odd prime (Schmidt; P18 M1)

Let D be an SDS (v, k, λ) on G, n = k − λ > 0, ZMod m a quotient of G with fibres of size c, p an odd prime with
p² ∣ m, and for every prime q ∣ n some e with 1 + m/p ≡ q^e (mod the q-free part of m). Then φ(m)·n ≤ m·(m/p)·c².
General form: p odd, p·F = m with p ∣ F, and for every prime q ∣ n some q^a ∣ m and e with 1 + F ≡ q^e (mod m/q^a).

**Uses.** C27's Parseval step, restated for invariant characters χ_a in place of C10's invariant coset sums
(`chi_eq_chi_restrict_of_chi`, `totient_mul_le_of_chi`).
**Evidence.** Schmidt's field descent (I10), one step: t = 1 + F has order p mod m; for each prime q ∣ n the
congruence puts t in the decomposition group of the primes over q, so σ_t fixes the ideal of χ_a(Y) and
χ_a(Y) = η·(fixed) with η^p = 1, η = ζ^(Fs); ζ^(−s)χ_a(Y) is fixed by σ_t, so it lies in the field of ζ^p-sums
and C27's Parseval bound applies with |S| ≤ F. P18's spike (`scratch/p18/report.md`) and the M1 helper
(`scratch/p18/m1-progress.md`): 60 rows killed (46 one-step rows of `scratch/p18/rows.out` and 14 that `rows.py` listed
as two-step chains but one step at p = 3 already kills); no `exists` row killed by any (m, p); the CRT condition of
`rows.py` agrees with a brute-force search in all 484 chain steps (`scratch/p18/crt_check.py`).
