### Restatement

C39: Let D be a (v, k, λ) signed difference set on G (n = k − λ, s² = k + λ(v − 1)), and H a Ma quotient of G as in C21/C34
(level 1) or C36 (level 2), with fibres of size c over H and the further quotient H′ (H with the p-part cut by p, resp. p²). The
coset sums W of D along G → H′ are divisible by p^l, and Z = W/p^l is an integer vector on H′ with |Z_h| ≤ c, ΣZ = ±s/p^l,
ΣZ² = Q := (k + λ(p^l c − 1))/p^(2l). If no multiset of |H′| integers in [−c, c] has that sum (either sign) and square sum, no SDS
exists. The claim is said to hold for every prime p, including p = 2.

C38: Under C34 at level 1, let M = ∏ q^(j_q) over primes q ≠ p with q^(2 j_q) | n, q ∤ |H′| and q self-conjugate mod exp H′.
Then all entries of Z are congruent mod M. If no vector meeting C34's demands (sum, square sum, autocorrelation, absolute-sum
bounds from a list valid for p = 2 too) has all entries ≡ Z_0 (mod M), no SDS exists.

### Finding 2 (check 1, C39's mathematics; recount of the 52 rows): SURVIVES

By hand. W = coset sums of D along G → H′ (fibres p^l c). C3 gives ΣW² = k + λ(p^l c − 1), ΣW·W(·+g) = λ p^l c (g ≠ 0),
(ΣW)² = s² = k + λ(v − 1), |W| ≤ p^l c, Σ|W| ≤ k with k − Σ|W| even. If p^l | W, Z = W/p^l is an integer vector with
|Z| ≤ c, ΣZ² = Q, ΣZ = ±s′, and these depend only on the value counts of Z; also ΣZ² ≡ ΣZ (mod 2) since Z(Z − 1) is even.
Nothing in this step uses p odd. The divisibility p | W (level 1) and p² | W (level 2) comes from `Ma.dvd_contract_quot` /
`Ma.dvd_contract_quot_sub` (`lean/SignedDifferenceSets/MaDivided.lean:152`, `MaDivided2.lean:27`), whose hypotheses are
`Fact p.Prime` and the C21 data only, no oddness, and they are Lean theorems with standard axioms (Finding 6); Ma's lemma is
stated for every prime p in the literature (Ma 1985). Empirically at p = 2 see Finding 4 (known sets) and Finding 5 (exists rows).
Recount (`R46/check_rows.py`, `min_nonzero`: exact DP over (square sum, sum) of the least number of nonzero
values in [−c, c]; zeros fill up to |H′|; no absolute-sum bound used): all 52 rows have no value counts. The reasons are
elementary: 29 rows have |s′| > Q (impossible, since |ΣZ| ≤ Σ|Z| ≤ ΣZ² for integers), 3 rows (N40375, N41125, N41305;
level 2, H′ = Z16) have Q − s′ odd, and 20 rows need more nonzero values than |H′| (e.g. N7450: Q = 117, s′ = 69,
c = 2: at least 45 nonzero values, |H′| = 44). Every row is p = 2 (28 at level 1, 24 at level 2), as the claim says. So
the claim's remark that the absolute-sum bound and the autocorrelation are not needed is confirmed. Example by hand, N5887
(SDS(160,40,4) in Z160): H = Z32 × Z5, 2² ≡ −1 mod 5, n = 36, b = 1, c = 1; W on Z16 × Z5 (fibres 2) is even with ΣW² = 44,
ΣW = ±26: W ∈ {0, ±2} gives 11 nonzero entries and |ΣW| ≤ 22 < 26.

### Finding 4 (check 3, C38's mathematics; the 14 rows recomputed): SURVIVES, with a hand proof of all 14

for each (q, i_q, j_q), C5 (`IsSDS.contract_congr_pow`, Turyn: q ∤ |H′|, q^(i_q) h = −h on H′, q^(2 j_q) | n) makes

q ≠ p and q ∤ |H′|. (ii) Negation and translation keep "constant mod M" (`false_of_noVectorResQ'`, line 167). (iii) The
bound list: from p Σ|Z| ≤ k and k − pΣ|Z| even, every possible A is covered by some k′ ≥ A with k′ ≡ A (mod 2); at p = 2
this needs both parities ([⌊k/2⌋, ⌊k/2⌋ − 1]), which the 7 p = 2 rows carry (Finding 1 rechecked the cover for all 14).
(iv) `noVectorRes`: first entry of largest magnitude (after translation), later entries |y| ≤ |x| and ≡ x (mod M);
`goRes_sound`/`noVectorRes_sound` are theorems.
Independent search (`R46/vsearch.rs`, own Rust code: index-order DFS, every residue class, both signs, no
translation or negation normalisation, no absolute-sum bound; prune: for every shift g the completed part of
Σ_y (Z(y) − Z(y+g))² = 2(Q − t) can only grow; every leaf rechecked against the full autocorrelation). Validation: N25970 with

rows: 0 vectors, 224 to 530 nodes each, none capped (`res14.out`).
Hand proof (new, `structural14.py`): in all 14 rows Q − t = M² (25 or 49). Then for every g ≠ 0, Σ_y (Z(y) − Z(y+g))² =
2M² with every difference a multiple of M, so exactly two y have Z(y) ≠ Z(y+g), each differing by ±M. Summing over g,

square sum then fix u (up to negation): the seven N = 12 rows (p = 3) have no integer u; the seven p = 2 rows force a
value u + εM = 8, 9, −6, 8, 7, 12, 12 (N25970, N26009, N31317, N46914, N46887, N61885, N61887) beyond c (e.g. N31317:
Z = 1 − 7δ_g, |−6| > c = 5). So every C38 row dies for an elementary reason
once Z is constant mod M; the computer search is not essential.

   counts; the reasons are elementary (|s′| > Q on 29, parity on 3, too many nonzero values on 20).
2. `countsGo_of` / `anyCountsMag_of`: SURVIVES. Proved completeness checked against the brief's points; a Python port
   agrees exactly with the DP on 4,000 random instances and is false on all 52 rows.
3. C38's mathematics: SURVIVES. Every step checked; all 14 rows also have a short hand proof (Q − t = M² forces
   Z = u ± Mδ_g, which violates the sum or |Z| ≤ c).
4. Rows and certificates: SURVIVES. All 66 row theorems state the table's rows; every certificate re-derived
   independently; settle lists consistent; no missed count kills among the 6,044 open rows. The residue is not needed for
   the seven p = 3 rows of C38 (C34 alone kills them with a larger search); for the seven p = 2 rows it is, as far as tested.
5. Lean: SURVIVES. Theorem types say what the claims say, non-vacuous on existing rows; 83 constants (17 core,
   66 rows) use only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; two rows recompiled from source.
6. Literature: C39's method is Arasu–Gordon–Zhang's for circulant weighing matrices (arXiv:1908.08447, Thm 2.1 and
   Lemma 1); Ma's lemma and Turyn's congruence are classical. Not found: the SDS statements, the combination with
   Turyn's residue class, the rows (Gordon: Open; He–Wu's four tests leave them open).

Overall: **SURVIVES** (C38 and C39). Known sets give no evidence for Ma's step at p = 2, since they satisfy p | W even

  ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1908.08447): Theorem 2.1 (if AA^(−1) ≡ 0 mod p^(2a) and p is self-conjugate, then A ≡ 0 mod p^a;
