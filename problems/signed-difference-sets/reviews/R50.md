### Statements in my words

- C44. Let p be prime and D an SDS (v, k, λ) on G with n = k − λ = p², and φ : G → Z_p × Z_p onto (fibres c = v/p²). Then
  s² = p²(1 + λc), so s = ±p·q with q² = 1 + λc. Each of the p + 1 line contractions Y (a vector on Z_p, fibres c·p) has
  autocorrelation p²·[g = 0] + λcp; its group-ring image a = ΣY(t)ζ^t has a·ā = p², and since p is totally ramified in
  ℚ(ζ_p), p ∣ a, i.e. Y is constant mod p. With ΣY = pq and ΣY² = pq² + p(p − 1), Y ≡ q ± 1 (mod p); if c·p < q + p − 1, the
  q − 1 case forces an entry ≥ q + p − 1 > c·p. The line identity at 0, Σ_j Y_j(0) = p·W(0) + s, then gives
  (p + 1)(q + 1) ≡ 0, i.e. p ∣ q + 1. Hence: c·p < q + p − 1 and p ∤ q + 1 imply no SDS. (Sign of s: D is replaced by −D.)
- C45. G of order 90 onto Z_2 × Z_3², z = contraction to Z_3² (fibres 10), d = its twist by the Z_2 character. z has
  autocorrelation n·δ + 10λ, d has n·δ; since 2 ≡ −1 (mod 3), Turyn's self-conjugacy makes both constant mod 2^j when
  2^(2j) ∣ n. With z ± d = 2·W(0/1, ·), |W| ≤ 5 and Σ|W| ≤ k, residue counting kills (90, 18, 2) (j = 2) and (90, 72, 8)

## Literature

- The constancy lemma is classical in substance. Turyn's self-conjugacy (prime ideals above a self-conjugate q are fixed by
  complex conjugation) and Ma's lemma (an element of Z[K], K with cyclic Sylow q-subgroup, whose nontrivial character
  values are divisible by q^a, has the form q^a·X₁ + Q·X₂, Q the subgroup of order q) give it at once for K = Z_p, q = p
  (p is self-conjugate mod p). Opened: O. Yayla, "Nearly perfect sequences with arbitrary out-of-phase autocorrelation",
  arXiv:1408.6883 (2014), Lemma 1 (Turyn) and Lemma 2 (Ma's lemma, crediting S. L. Ma and W. S. Ng, Int. J. Inf. Coding
  Theory 1 (2009)). Ma's original paper was not opened. C44's Lean proof (the total-ramification argument in 𝓞 ℚ(ζ_p)) is
  a direct proof of this special case; credit for the lemma: classical; for the line-tomography use and the rows: ours.
- C45: the mod-2^j constancy is C5 (Turyn's self-conjugacy, already credited there); applying it to the Z_2 twist on a
  Z_2 × Z_3² quotient and the two residue kills: no precedent found.
- He and Wu, arXiv:2608.22936 (v2; text extracted locally): their self-conjugate-prime obstruction (Theorem 3.2) needs
  v_p(n) odd; with n = p² (C44) or n = 16, 64 (C45) it does not apply, and the repository's import reproduces their open
  list with all 27 rows here still open. Gordon's database (github.com/dmgordo/signed-difference-sets, commits page opened
  2026-11-10): no commit after e3bf810 (2026-04-24), so no row here is settled there.
- Searches that found nothing on these rows (2026-11-10): "signed difference sets nonexistence 2026 arXiv"; "signed
  difference set (90,18,2) / k − λ = p² nonexistence … constant modulo p ramified". The second search also returned
  arXiv:2608.18468 (circulant weighing matrices, i.e. λ = 0 and cyclic groups; not opened, none of our rows is of that kind).
