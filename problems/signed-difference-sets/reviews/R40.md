### Restatement

D : G -> {0, ±1} on a finite abelian group G of order v, marking k elements, with signed autocorrelation
Σ_x D(x)D(x+g) = λ at every g ≠ 0; n = k − λ. Take a surjection φ : G -> H = Z_{rp} × K (p prime, r ≥ 1,
p^i ≡ −1 on K, i.e. p^i h = −h for all h in K), with fibres of size c = v/(rp|K|). If p^e | n and 1 ≤ b ≤ e/2
(so p² | n) and Z_r × K is not trivial, then p | λc.
Corollary used on the rows: if p² | n and the Sylow p-subgroup of G is cyclic of order p^j ≥ p², then
(H = Z_{p^j}, K = 1, r = p^{j−1} > 1, c = v/p^j prime to p) p | λ. A row with these data and p ∤ λ has no SDS.
Reading tested: the natural one above. One ambiguity only: "fibres of size c" is |G|/|H|, and the Lean takes it
as the hypothesis card G = c·card H; the two agree.

### Check 1a: the mathematics by hand (SURVIVES; one wording remark)

Derivation redone. In Z[G], D D^(−1) = n + λG (identity coefficient k = n + λ). Y = φ(D) on H = Z_{rp} × K has
Y Y^(−1) = n + λc·H. For a character χ of H nontrivial on P = <u>, u = (r, 0) of order p: χ(H) = 0, so |χ(Y)|² = n;
p is self-conjugate modulo the p'-part of exp H (p^i ≡ −1 on K, and r a power of p), so p^{2b} | n gives
χ(Y) ≡ 0 mod p^b (Turyn); Ma's lemma gives Y = p^b X1 + P X2, hence (1 − u)Y ≡ 0 mod p^b: Y(h + u) ≡ Y(h) mod p^b
(this is C21's step, Lean `Ma.dvd_contract_sub`, `lean/SignedDifferenceSets/MaDescent.lean:29`). Contract along
ψ : H -> H/P = Z_r × K: W(y) = Σ_{t<p} Y(h + tu) ≡ p·Y(h) ≡ 0 mod p (b ≥ 1). W W^(−1) = n + λpc·(H/P); at g ≠ 0,
Σ_y W(y)W(y+g) = λpc ≡ 0 mod p², so p | λc. Every step checks; cases p = 2 and p odd, e odd or even (b = ⌊e/2⌋),
K trivial or not are all covered by the same argument.
Remarks (none breaks the claim):
- The hypothesis "Z_r × K nontrivial" is not needed: the identity coefficient Σ_y W(y)² = n + λpc gives p² | λpc
  as well (with H/P trivial this is the s² = n + λv argument C33.md mentions).
- C33.md's text does not say r is a power of p; its proof (Ma's lemma on Z_{rp} × K) needs that (or self-conjugacy of
  p modulo r's p'-part). The Lean theorem has it through `CycloFacts p r` (which, per R23, forces r to be a power of p).
  The plain statement stays true for any r anyway: if p | c it is trivial; if p ∤ c, G's Sylow p-subgroup maps
  isomorphically onto H's, which is cyclic (|K| is prime to p since p^i + 1 kills K), and the corollary applies (order
  p by s², order ≥ p² by Ma). Suggest writing "r a power of p" in C33.md.
- Consequence: the general form (any K) kills exactly the rows the corollary kills (cyclic Sylow p of order ≥ p²,
  p² | n, p ∤ λ); a nontrivial K can never add a row. And for p = 2 the claim is empty: λ is even whenever v is even.

Check 3 (rows): SURVIVES. Independent recomputation: exactly the 151 open rows of settle.tsv, 0 `exists`,
1,869 `none`; 151 theorems match their table rows; 56 rows in non-cyclic groups correctly use the Sylow p factor.
Check 4 (literature): SURVIVES. Method classical (Ma, Turyn, the G/P step for ordinary difference sets); the SDS
statement p | λc and the 151 rows not found in print (He-Wu 2026 obstructions do not reach them; Gordon lists them
Open).

Overall: SURVIVES.
