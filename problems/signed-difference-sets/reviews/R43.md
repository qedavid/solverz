### Restatement

D : G → {0, ±1} on a finite abelian group G of order v with k nonzero entries and Σ_x D(x)D(x+g) = λ for all g ≠ 0;
n = k − λ, s = Σ D (s² = k + λ(v − 1)). Take p prime with p^e | n, 2 ≤ b ≤ e/2, a surjection φ : G → H = Z_{r p²} × K,
r a power of p (r ≥ 1), K with p^i h = −h for all h (so p ∤ |K|), c = v/|H|. Map further H → Z_{rp} × K → Z_r × K
(each step kills the subgroup of order p of the cyclic factor), so χ : G → H'' = Z_r × K has fibres p²c; let
W(y) = Σ_{χ(x)=y} D(x). Claim: p² | W(y) for every y, and Z = W/p² satisfies |Z| ≤ c; ΣZ² = Q with
p⁴Q = k + λ(p²c − 1); Σ_y Z(y)Z(y+g) = t with p²t = λc for g ≠ 0; ΣZ = ±s' with p²s' = s; A = Σ|Z| has p²A ≤ k and
k − p²A even. A bounds list k' (A ≤ k', k' − A even for some k' in the list, for every admissible A) turns this into a
finite search; if no Z exists for any listed bound, no SDS exists. For odd p one bound (k' = ⌊k/p²⌋ adjusted to k's
parity); for p = 2 the parity of A is free, so two bounds ⌊k/4⌋, ⌊k/4⌋ − 1.
Reading: "p^i h = −h on K" for some i ≥ 1; "C21 quotient" also requires r to be a power of p (Lean: `CycloFacts p (r p)`
and `CycloFacts p r`, from `maPairs`). No ambiguity affects the kills.

### Check 1: the mathematics (SURVIVES)

  Y(h + u) ≡ Y(h) (mod p^b). The coset sum below h is W₁(ψ₁h) = Σ_{m<p} Y(h + m u) ≡ p·Y(h) (mod p^b). With b ≥ 1,
  p | W₁. This is `Ma.dvd_contract_quot_sub` (`MaDivided2.lean:27`), proved by exactly this splitting (lines 68–81).
- Level 1 → 2. H₁ = Z_{rp} × K is again a C21 quotient of G (fibres pc; Sylow p cyclic, same K, same self-conjugacy,
  same p^e | n), and Ma's step does not depend on the fibre size: χ₁(D)χ₁(D)⁽⁻¹⁾ = n + λpc·H₁, so in Z[H₁]/(P₁) the
  product is n and the π-adic induction gives W₁(h₁ + u₁) ≡ W₁(h₁) (mod p^b) (Lean: the same lemma applied to χ₁ with
  `hcard1`, `CycloFacts p r`; `MaDivided2.lean:133`). Then W(ψ₂h₁) ≡ p·W₁(h₁) ≡ 0 (mod p²) because p | W₁ and b ≥ 2
  (lines 130–138). ✓ For r = 1 the second level is H₁ = Z_p × K, which C21 also covers (`CycloFacts p 1`).
- Demands on Z = W/p², fibres p²c: |W| ≤ p²c ⇒ |Z| ≤ c; ΣW² = k + λ(p²c − 1) ⇒ p⁴Q = k + λ(p²c − 1); for g ≠ 0,
  ΣW(y)W(y+g) = λp²c ⇒ p⁴t = λp²c ⇒ p²t = λc; ΣW = s ⇒ ΣZ = s/p² (the Lean derives (ΣZ)² = s'² from ΣW², the
  autocorrelation and hs, lines 204–219); Σ|W| ≤ k with Σ|W| ≡ k (mod 2) (each nonzero D(x) is odd) ⇒ p²A ≤ k,
  k − p²A even. ✓
- Bounds. Odd p: p²A ≡ A (mod 2), so A ≡ k (mod 2) and A ≤ ⌊k/p²⌋: one bound of k's parity covers it. p = 2: k − 4A
  even only says k is even (if k is odd there is no admissible A and the theorem rightly says no SDS); A is any integer in
  [0, ⌊k/4⌋], and {⌊k/4⌋, ⌊k/4⌋ − 1} with their parities cover exactly that range. The Lean asks the covering as a decidable
  hypothesis over A ∈ range(k+1) (`hbounds`, line 95), so a wrong bounds list cannot pass. ✓
- Sign and translation are handled by C34's `false_of_noVectorQ'` (R42). ✓
