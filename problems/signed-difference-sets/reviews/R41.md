### Restatement

An SDS D on a finite abelian group G, n = k − λ > 0, and a surjection G → ZMod m (fibres of size c = v/m) give coset sums
f : ZMod m → ℤ, |f| ≤ c, and X = Σ f(h) ζ_m^h ∈ ℤ[ζ_m] with X X̄ = n. A step d → d/p (d ∣ m) is valid when p² ∣ d,
p is odd or p = 2 with 8 ∣ d, every prime of m divides d/p, and for every prime q ∣ n some unit t (mod m) has
t ≡ 1 + d/p (mod d) and t ≡ q^e modulo m/q^a for some q^a ∣ m (equivalently, a = v_q(m): t mod m_q ∈ ⟨q⟩, m_q the q-free
part). Along a chain of valid steps m = d₀ → … → F, some ζ^j X lies in ℚ(ζ_F) (fixed by U_F = {σ_t : t ≡ 1 mod F}), and
then φ(m)·n ≤ m·F·c². A row is killed when some cyclic quotient and chain give m·F·c² < φ(m)·n.
Ambiguity: C35's text says "pᵢ² ∣ dᵢ" without "pᵢ prime"; the Lean `DescentStep` indeed does not need primality (p odd
or 2), and the certificates require `IsPrimeTD p`, so the rows rest on prime p only. Nothing else is ambiguous.

### Check 1: the statement says what Schmidt's field descent says: SURVIVES

Read literally (`FieldDescentChain.lean` lines 34–44, 381–385):
- `DescentStep m n d p`: `p * (d / p) = d ∧ p ∣ d / p` is p² ∣ d (for p > 0; p = 0 is impossible since d ∣ m, m > 0);
  `Odd p ∨ (p = 2 ∧ 8 ∣ d)`; `∀ r prime, r ∣ m → r ∣ d / p` is rad m ∣ d/p; for each prime q ∣ n,
  `∃ t a e, t.Coprime m ∧ t ≡ 1 + d/p [MOD d] ∧ q^a ∣ m ∧ t ≡ q^e [MOD m / q^a]`. With a = v_q(m) (the weakest choice:
  m/q^a is then the q-free part m_q and every other admissible m/q^a is a multiple of it) this is exactly C35's text
  "t ≡ q^e modulo the q-free part of m". By CRT (rad m ∣ d makes every t ≡ 1 + d/p mod d a unit) it is equivalent to
  "1 + d/p ∈ ⟨q⟩ in (ℤ/d_q)ˣ", i.e. σ_{1+d/p} restricted to ℚ(ζ_d) lies in the decomposition group of q: the right
  condition for Schmidt's descent from ℚ(ζ_d) to ℚ(ζ_{d/p}).
- `DescentChain m n m ps` starts at d₀ = m and F = `ps.foldl (·/·) m`; the bound is
  `φ(m)·(k − λ) ≤ m·F·c²` in ℤ with `Fintype.card G = c * m` and φ : G →+ ZMod m onto, `0 < k − λ`, `NeZero m`, 1 < m.
  No junk: ℕ division only in d/p (exact, by the first conjunct) and m/q^a (exact, q^a ∣ m); n enters as
  `((k : ℤ) − lam).toNat` with 0 < k − λ, so it is k − λ.
- Degenerate steps: p = 1 is a valid `DescentStep` (1 is odd, t = 1), a no-op that leaves F unchanged; odd composite p
