### Restatement

(a) If D : ZMod 2 × K → {−1, 0, 1} is an SDS (v, k, λ) (DD* = k + λ(G − 1) in ℤ[G]), K odd with 2 self-conjugate
(2^i ≡ −1 mod exp K), and 4 ∣ k − λ, then Z = (D(0,·) + D(1,·))/2 and T = (D(0,·) − D(1,·))/2 are ±1/0-valued with
disjoint supports, Z is an SDS ((k + λ)/4, λ/2) and T an SDS ((k − λ)/4, 0) (a circulant-type weighing matrix) on K.
(b) For v = 54, k = 46, λ = 10 on ZMod 3 × ZMod 18 there is no SDS. Via (a) with K = C₃ × C₉ (2 has order 6 mod 9,
2³ = 8 ≡ −1): no pair (Z SDS (27, 14, 5), T SDS (27, 9, 0)) with disjoint supports exists.

- The c = 1 split for weighing matrices is in print: Schmidt–Smith, *Circulant weighing matrices whose order and
  weight are products of powers of 2 and 3* (JCTA 120 (2013); preprint
  [weighing23.pdf](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/bernhard/Publications/pub/weighing23.pdf), opened 2026-11-10),
  Lemma 6.2: for a CW(v, 36), 2 self-conjugate mod v, Ma's Lemma gives D = 2A + (1 + g)B and then
  D·h = (1 − g)X + (1 + g)Y with X, Y ∈ {0, ±1}, supp X on distinct ⟨g⟩-cosets, and ρ(Y) a CW(v/2, 9). That is the
  λ = 0 case of C37(a) with both sides (their (1 + g)Y is our Z, their (1 − g)X our T; for a 2-part of order 2,
  |χ(X)|² = 36/4 for χ(g) = −1 makes X a weight-9 weighing matrix on K, the T-side). For SDS with λ ≠ 0 the split
  (Z an SDS ((k+λ)/4, λ/2) **and** T an SDS ((k−λ)/4, 0)) I found nowhere; C26 already credits the halving to
  Schmidt–Smith via Tan's survey. The converse (doubling) credit in I21 (Arasu–Leung–Ma–Nabavi–Ray-Chaudhuri 2006
  Thm 2.3 via Arasu–Kotsireas–Koukouvinos–Seberry 2010 Thm 4) I could not open (paywalled / encrypted PDFs).
- Credit suggestion (not a flaw in C37's mathematics): C37's Origin line should say "the c = 1 split D = (1 − g)X +
  (1 + g)Y is Schmidt–Smith 2013 Lemma 6.2 for weighing matrices (λ = 0); its extension to SDS with λ ≠ 0, with both
  halves, and the N464 search: us".
