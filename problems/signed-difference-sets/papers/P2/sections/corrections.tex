\section{Corrections to the database}\label{sec:corrections}

The \corrections{} cases in Table~\ref{tab:corrections} are listed as open in the
database~\cite{gordon-db}, although published constructions settle them. We found them while
checking published constructions against the open cases; for each we give a set checked in
Lean. The list is not complete (Section~\ref{sec:open}).

  (Paley and zero) \\

For N14471, Theorem~4.1 of~\cite{he-chen-ge} needs $x_1 = 0$: for $x_1 \neq 0$, as in their
Example~4.2 ($x_1 = (1,0)$), the construction does not give a signed set.
