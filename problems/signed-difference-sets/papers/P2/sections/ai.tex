\section*{Use of AI}

I did this work with AI tools (Anthropic's Claude models). They searched the literature,
found and proved the nonexistence criteria, ran the searches, wrote the Lean proofs and drafted
this paper; I designed the workflow, directed the work and decided which results to keep. So
that nothing rests on the AI alone, every case we settle or correct is checked by Lean~4, with
no unproved steps and no axioms beyond the three standard ones, and before a result was
accepted a separate AI agent tried to break it: its proof, its Lean statement, the cases it
settles and its credit to earlier work. Results proved only on paper are marked as such in the
text. The Lean statements are the authoritative form of every case we settle or correct; all
code, proofs, data and review reports are public (\repo).
