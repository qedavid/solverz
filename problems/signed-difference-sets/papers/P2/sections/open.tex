\section{What is still open}\label{sec:open}

\stillOpen{} cases remain open in the database after our results:
\csname openBelow50\endcsname{} with $v < 50$, \csname openBelow100\endcsname{} with
$50 \le v < 100$, \csname openBelow200\endcsname{} with $100 \le v < 200$ and
\csname openBelow500\endcsname{} with $200 \le v < 500$. Roughly, they fall into four kinds:
cases where a multiplier applies but the orbit search is too large; cases where one of the
criteria above fails by a small margin; $p$-groups with $n$ a power of $p$, where a construction
seems more likely; and a core where no multiplier applies and every congruence modulus is below
$c$. We expect most of the core to admit no signed difference set, but none of the local
arguments above can show it: settling it seems to need conditions that combine many quotients
at once.
